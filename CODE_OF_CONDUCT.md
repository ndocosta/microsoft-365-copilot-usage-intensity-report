# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participants should communicate respectfully, assume good intent, accept
constructive feedback, and focus discussions on the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disruption, publication of another person's private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is
inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on
the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially.
